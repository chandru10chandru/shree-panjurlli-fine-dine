Character of the House

A dining room with a story.

“More than just another restaurant, Shree Panjurlli was built to feel like an inspiring gathering place for families, friends, and travelers crossing through Belagavi.”

When you step into our dining space at Kuber Plaza, you notice an atmosphere unlike any ordinary eatery. Our entrance is anchored by a hand-carved stone reception counter sculpted with reliefs of elephants, lions, and peacocks reminiscent of temple architecture, bathed in warm backlit amber light.

Above, circular geometric coffered wooden ceilings frame private, cushioned family booths partitioned by custom teak jaali screens. And on our walls, an expansive curated gallery pays tribute to India's extraordinary achievers, social reformers, environmentalists, and thinkers.

Heritage Craftsmanship

Carved stone reception columns, geometric wooden woodwork, and vintage patterned ceramic floors.

Air-conditioned family dining enclosures, intimate booth seating, and dedicated party banquet spaces.
